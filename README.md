## Short Description
Traits is a datapack for Minecraft that adds a handful of different mob traits
you can choose from, each with their own abilities, benefits, and drawbacks.

In addition to the listed effects, whichever set of traits you choose will also
make that mob passive towards you.

Also endermites are annoying, so they are completely disabled when this datapack
is active.

## List Of Traits
### Blaze
#### Abilities
- Shoot Fireballs

#### Benefits
- Fire resistance

#### Drawbacks
- Damaged by water
- Slightly less health

### Cow
#### Abilities
- Place bucket in offhand to get milk

#### Benefits
- Bread give you saturation
- Gain speed when hit
- More hand knockback

#### Drawbacks
- Slightly less health

### Creeper
#### Abilities
- Explode

#### Benefits
- Night vision

#### Drawbacks
- You die when exploding

### Enderman
#### Abilities
- Infinite Ender Pearls

#### Benefits
- Gain regeneration after teleporting

#### Drawbacks
- Damaged by water

### Human
#### Benefits
- All recipies are unlocked

### Iron Golem
#### Benefits
- Farther reach
- Less knockback
- More health
- More hand damage
- Hand knockback

#### Drawbacks
- More gravity
- Slower speed
- Slower hand attack

### Phantom
#### Abilities
- Can glide

#### Benefits
- Night vision
- Immune to poison

#### Drawbacks
- Immune to regeneration
- Burns in daylight

### Shulker
#### Abilities
- Shoot bullets

#### Benefits
- Fire resistance
- Immune to levitation

#### Drawbacks
- Damaged by water
- 25% chance to randomly teleport when damaged

### Skeleton
#### Abilities
- Infinite Skeleton Arrows

#### Benefits
- Immune to poison
- Receive a bow when adopting traits

#### Drawbacks
- Immune to regeneration
- Burns in daylight

### Spider
#### Abilities
- Hold jump to climb walls
- Poison any mob you touch

#### Benefits
- Immune to poison

#### Drawbacks
- Slightly less health

### Snow Golem
#### Abilities
- Throw Snowballs

#### Benefits
- Immune to fall damage
- Receive a carved pumpkin when adopting traits

#### Drawbacks
- Spreads snow
- Damaged by daylight, hot biomes, and water

### Zombie
#### Abilities
- Zombify or poison any mob you touch
- Summon more zombies

#### Benefits
- Immune to poison

#### Drawbacks
- Immune to regeneration
- Burns in daylight
